# Build your shop's first app with AI: 7 reel scripts

For small business owners who don't code. Each reel is about 60 seconds: shot vertically, Kamal to camera, with his screen shown where it says SCREEN. Spoken in Hinglish. The recall question comes on screen at the end and is answered at the start of the next reel.

Task: `topc-social/vidyam-tasks#4`. Topics follow the 2025 Vidyam AI bootcamp classes.

---

## 1. Aapki dukaan ke liye AI kya bana sakta hai

**Hook:** "Apni dukaan ka app banane ke liye ab developer dhoondhne ki zaroorat nahi."

**Say:**
- Aaj aise tools hain, jaise Lovable, jahan aap Hindi ya English mein likhte ho ki kya chahiye, aur AI screen bana deta hai.
- Ye achha banata hai: order form, booking page, product catalogue, customers ki list.
- Abhi isse bachke: jahan galti ka matlab paisa hai, jaise payments. Wahan kisi jaankaar se check karwao.
- Shuruaat ek page se karo, jo ek problem solve kare.

**SCREEN:** a finished one-page booking form.

**Question:** Pehle app mein kitni problems solve karni chahiye?

---

## 2. AI ko kaise samjhayein kya banana hai

**Hook:** "AI wahi banata hai jo aap bolte ho. Galat bologe, galat banega."

**Say:**
- Teen cheezein batao: kaun use karega, woh kya karega, aur aapko kya dikhna chahiye.
- Example: "Mere salon ke liye booking page. Customer naam, phone, service aur time chunega. Mujhe saari bookings ki list dikhe."
- Ye mat bolo: "Mujhe Amazon jaisa app chahiye." Isse AI ko kuch samajh nahi aata.

**SCREEN:** the salon prompt typed out.

**Question:** AI ko kaunsi teen cheezein batani hain?

---

## 3. Pehla page banao, ek badlaav ek baar mein

**Hook:** "Pehla version 5 minute mein dikh jaata hai. Asli kaam uske baad hai."

**Say:**
- Lovable kholo, apna description paste karo, aur preview dekho.
- Jo pasand nahi, saade shabdon mein bolo: "Button hara karo", "Phone number zaroori karo."
- Ek baar mein ek hi badlaav maango. Paanch ek saath maangoge to kuch na kuch toot jaayega, aur pata nahi chalega kya.

**SCREEN:** prompt, preview, one change, new preview.

**Question:** Ek baar mein kitne badlaav maangne chahiye?

---

## 4. Booking aayi, par gayi kahaan?

**Hook:** "Form bhar diya, par booking kahin save hi nahi hui. Ye sabse common galti hai."

**Say:**
- Page ko yaad rakhne ke liye database chahiye. Lovable mein database jodne ka option hai.
- AI ko bolo: "Har booking database mein save karo, aur mujhe ek admin page par list dikhao."
- Admin page par login zaroor lagwao. Warna koi bhi aapke customers ke phone number dekh lega.

**SCREEN:** a booking going in, then showing up on the admin list.

**Question:** Admin page par login kyun zaroori hai?

---

## 5. Live karne se pehle khud customer bano

**Hook:** "Link share karne se pehle ek kaam karo, warna pehla customer hi bug dhoondhega."

**Say:**
- Publish karte hi aapko ek link milta hai. Wahi WhatsApp par ya Google profile par daal sakte ho.
- Share karne se pehle apne phone se khud ek booking karo, jaise customer karega.
- Check karo: booking admin list mein aayi? Phone par sab theek dikh raha hai?
- Apna domain baad mein bhi le sakte ho, uska alag se saalana kharcha hota hai.

**SCREEN:** publish, open the link on a phone, make a test booking.

**Question:** Link share karne se pehle kya karna hai?

---

## 6. Booking aate hi aapko message

**Hook:** "Har ghante app khol ke check karna band karo."

**Say:**
- Automation ke do hisse hote hain: trigger, yaani kya hua, aur action, yaani phir kya karna hai.
- Trigger: nayi booking aayi. Action: aapko email ya message chala jaaye.
- Zapier, Make ya n8n jaise tools ye jodte hain. Hamari class mein yahi kiya tha: email aaya, AI ne padha, jawab chala gaya.

**SCREEN:** a new booking, then the notification arriving.

**Question:** Automation ke do hisse kaunse hain?

---

## 7. Jab app toot jaaye

**Hook:** "Aapka app tootega. Sabka tootta hai. Farak ye hai ki aap use theek kaise karwaate ho."

**Say:**
- Ghabraana nahi. Error ka screenshot lo.
- AI ko teen cheezein batao: aapne kya kiya, kya hona chahiye tha, aur kya hua.
- Ek baar mein ek fix. Aur jo version chal raha tha, use wapas laane ka option hota hai: use karo.

**SCREEN:** the error, the three-part message to AI, the fix.

**Question:** AI ko bug batate waqt kaunsi teen cheezein batani hain?

---

## Answers

1. Ek. 2. Kaun use karega, woh kya karega, aapko kya dikhe. 3. Ek. 4. Customers ka data private rahe. 5. Khud ek test booking. 6. Trigger aur action. 7. Kya kiya, kya hona tha, kya hua.

## Before recording

- Check each tool step on the day you record. Lovable's database option, publishing and version restore were true as far as known, but tools change fast.
- No prices or numbers are in the scripts on purpose. Don't add any that can't be shown.
